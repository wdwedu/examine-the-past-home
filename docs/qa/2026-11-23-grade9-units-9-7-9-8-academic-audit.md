## Academic safeguarding
- Treat institutional historical explanations, source catalogs, and original documents as different genres.
- Avoid false neutrality over Holocaust facts: settled evidence of Nazi genocide is not a two-sides debate. Appropriate inquiry questions concern chronology, perpetrator decisions, documentation and human experiences.
- Avoid invented victim testimony, unverified casualty figures and simplistic single-cause accounts of Japanese surrender, Pearl Harbor, Korean War, Vietnam or Cold War beginnings.
- For mass violence and internment, choose age-appropriate testimony and avoid graphic sensationalism.
- These records are source-selection and editorial improvements, not a claim that every original archival artifact has been transcribed and checked line by line.
