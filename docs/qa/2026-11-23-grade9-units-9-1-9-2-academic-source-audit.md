## Method and verification boundary
References were selected using the U.S. National Archives, Library of Congress catalog/search records, UCSB American Presidency Project text, and Project Gutenberg's original-work edition. National Archives named-document pages and the 1892 party platform were inspected; the NARA suffrage collection page supplies a direct item catalog link. Some LOC item pages and Gutenberg pages blocked direct web fetching despite search-indexed title/metadata, so item-level browser rendering and detailed passage collation remain to be completed before classroom publication. This report does **not** describe the entire texts as fully read or certify every paragraph claim.
