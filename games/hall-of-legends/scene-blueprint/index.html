Historical research and layout information
Hall of Legends · Interactive Museum Layout

The Chronological Ascent

Explore 141 historical figures in order of their passing, ascending from Queen Makeda's traditional era to James Earl Jones (2024). The gallery architecture is still a layout study; detailed museum backgrounds will be designed around these portrait positions.

How to inspect: Start at Queen Makeda and scroll upward. Tap a portrait to illuminate and straighten it; select the separate Bio link to open its biography preview. The central passage is reserved for exhibits and eight Era Challenges.

Historical geography: This gallery is ordered by year of death, not the year of each person's greatest accomplishment. Ancient figures without verified death dates use explicitly labeled approximate or traditional placements. Museum-wall themes will represent the historical contexts of the figures, not imply that their lives took place in one location.
