Research and historical integrity

We prioritize historians and scholars from Africa and the African diaspora, as well as historical records. Every eventual biography and question will have its own source citations. The sequence below uses preliminary historical activity or contribution dates for placement—not necessarily birth dates. Displayed dates are working labels and still require individual source verification.

Traditional and contested figures are labeled accordingly. The modern illustrations in the uploaded collections are artistic interpretations; they are not automatically historical portraits.
