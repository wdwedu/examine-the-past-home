Follow all four stages or jump directly to the one you need. The course teaches test-day strategy; the three 120-question assessments will use separate, historically reviewed question banks.

01 • INTERACTIVE COURSE

Praxis History Preparation Course

Learn test-day planning, question strategy, evidence analysis, timing and review through short interactive scenarios.

02 • GUIDED PRACTICE

The History Foundations Lab

120 original questions with explanations and feedback by historical period. Your work can be saved and resumed.

03 • TIMED SIMULATION

The History Readiness Challenge

120 different questions in 120 minutes. A single uninterrupted attempt, with results and study recommendations afterwards.

04 • FINAL SIMULATION

The Certification Final Simulation

120 fresh questions in a second uninterrupted 120-minute examination, followed by a completion certificate and results report.
