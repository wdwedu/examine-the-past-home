Where AI Can Help

Start with a small, useful task. Review the result before putting it in front of learners.

Useful Starting Points

  • Brainstorm examples, questions and lesson openings.
  • Make directions clearer.
  • Draft practice questions you can check and adapt.
  • Build a first draft of a rubric or checklist.
  • Explore another explanation when learners are stuck.

Do Not Outsource

  • Your final check of historical facts and sources.
  • Final grading decisions.
  • Decisions about student welfare or discipline.
  • The thinking students are meant to practice.
  • Decisions about sharing private information.

Historical Work Needs Extra Care

A fluent answer can still contain a made-up date, quotation or source. Open the original material and check the claim. Use archives, museums, reliable scholarship and other sources you can actually inspect.

Ask whose perspective is missing, too. An answer can be incomplete even when its individual facts are correct.

Prompt Design

A Better Planning Prompt

Tell the AI what you need, who it is for and what a useful answer should look like.

1 · Task2 · Learners3 · Goal4 · Sources5 · Limits6 · Review
Help me plan a Grade 8 history activity about the Constitutional Convention. Students should compare two competing ideas about representation. I have 45 minutes and want a short evidence-based discussion. Use the source excerpts I provide. Include a vocabulary support and a three-question exit ticket. Flag anything that needs checking. Do not invent quotations or citations.

A helpful follow-up: “The reading is too dense. Keep the same question, shorten the directions and model one example.”

Academic Integrity

Disclose. Verify. Own Your Thinking.

Make expectations clear before the work begins. Students should know which help is allowed and which thinking they need to do themselves.

Disclose

Explain what AI helped you do. Follow the assignment’s rules for acknowledging that help. Keep a note of the prompts and changes that mattered.

Verify

Check generated claims against sources you can open and inspect. A citation that looks convincing is not enough.

Own Your Thinking

Be ready to explain your evidence, reasoning and choices. Use help to strengthen your work while keeping the decisions and understanding your own.
