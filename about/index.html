Our purpose

Examine the Past helps learners, teachers, and curious visitors investigate historical questions through courses, maps, timelines, biographies, interactive experiences, and classroom resources.

We emphasize evidence, chronology, geography, context, and more than one historical perspective. Course units and lesson previews distinguish planned experiences from completed resources.
