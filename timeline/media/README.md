# Timeline deeper learning

The ordered topics join the 100 timeline cards by display position and exact card title. Each has a selected video, topic-specific visual, evidence question, and source reading. `scripts/check_timeline_details.py` checks that these joins stay intact.

YouTube titles, channels, and public embed metadata were checked through the publisher's YouTube oEmbed response on November 13, 2026. Failed/private/deleted candidates were excluded. This is an educational selection, not a claim to have ranked every video by views or watched every film in full. Broader-context selections are labeled in the interface. External playback remains subject to publisher availability and school-network access.

September 11 uses the classroom film embedded by the 9/11 Memorial & Museum in its grades 6–12 “What Happened on 9/11?, Part I” lesson, plus a BBC Newsround explainer. Its chronology comes from the museum's published six-event chronology.

The unemployment chart uses the Federal Reserve History essay “The Great Recession”: 5% (December 2007), 9.5% (June 2009), and 10% (October 2009). It is labeled as selected observations, with a zero baseline and explicit units. It replaces unmeasured ten-point scores. The COVID chronology follows WHO's dated declarations; the end of the emergency designation is explicitly distinguished from the disappearance of the disease.

Other diagrams summarize relationships described in the card's linked historical sources. They are labeled as schematic comparisons/connections rather than quantitative measurements or inevitable causal sequences.

The three geographic panels use Natural Earth's public-domain 1:110m land outlines, from `nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson`. Coordinate precision is reduced for small browser maps. Named reference hubs and dashed connections are schematic, not reconstructed travel itineraries, historical borders, or distance measurements. Historical context links to UNESCO's Silk Roads programme.

Only the selected player's iframe is loaded after a play click. Selecting another video or closing the dialog destroys the previous player. Every film also has a publisher/watch-page link. The existing source context and activity choices remain in place.
