Start With the Question

Essential Question: How can a map support a historical claim—and how can a map mislead us?

Maps look authoritative, but every map involves choices about what to include, what to emphasize, and how to represent a curved Earth on a flat surface.

4 · Build Context

Historical Background

Maps are models of space. Historical maps can reveal political borders, trade routes, migration, settlement, resources, conflict, and perceptions of territory. But maps also reflect the purposes, knowledge, and priorities of their creators.

Context connection

A map is both information and an argument about what deserves attention.
