4 · Build Context

Historical Background

The Constitution intentionally gives institutions overlapping powers. Congress legislates and controls appropriations; the president executes laws and can veto; courts resolve cases and interpret law. Appointments, impeachment, confirmations, and overrides create additional checks.

Context connection

A check is not an automatic outcome. Political actors choose whether and how to use available constitutional powers.
