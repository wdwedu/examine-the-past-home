Start With the Question

Essential Question: How did the Columbian Exchange transform societies on both sides of the Atlantic—and why were its effects so unequal?

After 1492, organisms and people moved across the Atlantic on a scale that reshaped diets, economies, populations, landscapes, and empires.

Warm-up prompt

Can the same historical process create major benefits for some communities while causing catastrophe for others?

4 · Build Context

Historical Background

European voyages linked previously separated ecosystems. American crops spread widely; Eurasian animals and plants transformed American environments; Old World diseases devastated many Indigenous communities; and Atlantic empires expanded systems of coerced labor and slavery.

Context connection

The phrase 'exchange' can sound neutral, but many movements occurred under conquest, coercion, and unequal power.
