4 · Build Context

Historical Background

Water, fertile land, timber, minerals, energy, and transportation routes have influenced settlement throughout history. Political systems, technology, trade, and inequality affect who controls and benefits from resources.

Why this matters

A resource is not automatically valuable; its importance depends on technology, demand, access, and power.
