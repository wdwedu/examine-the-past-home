4 · Build Context

Historical Background

Environmental stress can affect food, water, housing, health, and livelihoods. Communities may adapt locally, migrate temporarily, relocate permanently, or combine strategies.

Why this matters

Climate is one factor among many; economic resources, government policy, social networks, and conflict also shape outcomes.
