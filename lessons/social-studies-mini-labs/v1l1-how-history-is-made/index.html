4 · Build Context

Historical Background

Historical knowledge is constructed from traces of the past—documents, objects, images, oral histories, buildings, statistics, and other evidence. Historians compare sources and explain uncertainty rather than pretending the record is complete.

Why this matters

Strong historical thinking means being willing to revise a conclusion when better evidence appears.
