4 · Build Context

Historical Background

Geographic position shapes access to resources, trade routes, water, fertile land, defensible terrain, and neighboring societies. Human decisions and institutions determine how those conditions are used.

Why this matters

Geography influences history; it does not mechanically produce one inevitable outcome.
