Curriculum Design Analysis — Expand

Strength: Essential questions, geographic and historical inquiry, sourcing, disciplinary literacy, coherent scope and formative checks can support deep learning. Design consideration: grade placement, unit sequencing, approved materials, required outcomes and specific indicator codes must be reconciled with current Maryland frameworks and Charles County planning documents before district use.

Individual lesson-to-indicator mapping and a full implementation audit remain in development. This is not district-approved or formally accredited curriculum.
