Challenges Facing the New Republic

The 1790s government faced public debt, disputes over taxation, and fears about foreign conflict. The Whiskey Rebellion in western Pennsylvania tested federal authority over an excise tax, while Washington's administration pursued neutrality during war between Britain and France.
