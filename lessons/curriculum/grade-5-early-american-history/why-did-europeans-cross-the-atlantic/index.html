Why Did Europeans Cross the Atlantic?

In the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries, European rulers and merchants sought profitable trade, power, and new routes to goods in Asia and Africa. Navigational techniques, ships, and maritime knowledge developed through exchanges across cultures, enabling longer voyages.
