Grade 5 · Indigenous North America

Indigenous Nations, Cultures, and Communities

The Haudenosaunee Confederacy brought distinct nations together in a political alliance, while Pueblo communities of the Southwest developed other institutions and practices. Neither example stands for all Indigenous peoples. Before colonization, North America contained many languages, governments, spiritual traditions, and trade networks.
