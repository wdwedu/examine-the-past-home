GRADE 5 · END-OF-UNIT ASSESSMENT

Loading unit assessment…

Ten auto-graded questions, explanatory feedback, and a separate teacher-scored written historical argument. No account, server submission, or required time limit.

How this works

Complete all ten questions before a proficiency decision is shown. Suggested benchmark: 8/10, plus satisfactory written argument. Sources and answers remain in your browser; no grade is sent to a teacher. These questions are an independent curriculum check and not a standardized exam.

Historical evidence performance task

Write a claim supported by two historical facts or source details. Include reasoning, a second perspective, and one evidence limitation.

Teacher rubric · 0–3 points each

Accuracy; relevant evidence; reasoning; acknowledgment of source limitations or missing perspectives. Suggested writing mastery: 9/12 points. Human review required. Alternative oral response permitted with the same criteria.
