Grade 5 · The American Revolution

Turning Points of the Revolutionary War

The American Revolution included battles, diplomacy, logistics, and civilian hardship. Victories at Trenton and Princeton in late 1776 and early 1777 helped restore morale, while the American victory at Saratoga in 1777 strengthened prospects for a French alliance.
