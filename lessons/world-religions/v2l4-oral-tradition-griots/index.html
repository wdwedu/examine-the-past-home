Essential Question: How can oral traditions preserve knowledge across generations?

4 · Build Context

Historical Background

Oral historians in many societies preserve genealogy, political memory, moral teaching, praise poetry, law, and cultural identity. In parts of West Africa, jeliw or griots are especially associated with this work.

Why this matters

Oral sources are neither automatically unreliable nor automatically exact; they require contextual analysis like written sources.
