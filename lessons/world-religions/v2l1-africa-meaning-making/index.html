4 · Build Context

Historical Background

Africa contains some of the deepest archaeological records of human development. Evidence of symbolic behavior includes pigments, ornaments, burials, markings, and repeated practices, though scholars debate specific meanings.

Why this matters

Historians should avoid projecting modern religious categories backward without evidence.
