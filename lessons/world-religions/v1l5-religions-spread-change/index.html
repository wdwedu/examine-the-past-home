4 · Build Context

Historical Background

Religions spread through peaceful exchange, migration, family networks, institutions, state support, missionary activity, conquest, and media. New settings can produce new interpretations and practices.

Why this matters

Change does not automatically mean loss of identity; traditions often negotiate continuity and adaptation.
