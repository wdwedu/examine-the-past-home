Start With the Question

Essential Question: Why did many British colonists move from protest to revolution?

In the 1760s, most colonists still considered themselves British. Within little more than a decade, thirteen colonies declared independence. The change was not caused by one event.

Warm-up prompt

What usually makes people stop trying to reform a system and decide to break away from it?

4 · Build Context

Historical Background

After 1763, Britain tried to raise revenue and tighten imperial control. Colonists challenged taxes, enforcement, and limits on self-government. Protest escalated through boycotts, resistance, violence, and competing claims about rights and authority.

Context connection

Historians distinguish long-term causes—such as political expectations and imperial structure—from short-term triggers such as particular taxes, confrontations, and military actions.
