4 · Build Context

Historical Background

The Constitution distributes federal power among legislative, executive, and judicial institutions. It also creates overlapping powers so each branch can limit the others. The design reflects concerns about both weak government and concentrated authority.

Context connection

Separation of powers divides responsibilities; checks and balances gives branches tools to restrain one another. The two ideas work together but are not identical.
