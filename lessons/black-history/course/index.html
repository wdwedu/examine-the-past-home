A Journey Through Time

A connected historical course exploring African societies, the African diaspora, the Americas, liberation struggles, achievements and the modern world.

Course Roadmap · In Development

Understand the Bigger Story

Unlike the Hall of Legends, which lets you freely explore individuals, the course will guide learners through historical developments in sequence, using maps, primary sources, interpretations, and short assessments. The outline is available now; individual course units are still being researched and built.

Two related historical lessons are already available: The Great Migration and The Harlem Renaissance. They are independent lessons—not a completed chronological course.

Historical research foundation

Curriculum development will prioritize African and African American historians and primary sources, including UNESCO's General History of Africa and the Smithsonian National Museum of African American History and Culture.
