Digital Products & Refunds

How optional downloadable products and third-party purchases relate to the free Examine the Past website.

Free website and optional products

History Hub previews and other public educational pages are free to browse. Optional downloadable games, image packs, and classroom products are showcased through outbound links to external marketplaces rather than processed through an on-site checkout.

Delivery and refund policies

The product listing and marketplace that processes a purchase controls the exact delivery method, eligibility for refunds, and support procedures. Please review the current listing and the marketplace policy before buying. The Examine the Past website does not itself provide the marketplace order or receipt.
